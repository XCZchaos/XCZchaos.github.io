CT-MIFNet: Convolutional transformer-based multi-view interaction and fusion network for EEG decoding

Yibo Xiong, Jinming Li, Yun Zhuang, Xiangyue Zhao, Yilu Xu, Lilin Jie

Biomedical Signal Processing and Control · Volume 112 · Article 108421

CT-MIFNet combines spatial transformation, multi-scale temporal-frequency-spatial convolution, and Transformer-based feature interaction. Its Cross-Covariance Attention mechanism reduces the cost of long EEG sequences while enabling effective multi-view information exchange and fusion.
